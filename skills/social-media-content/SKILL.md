# Social Media Content Generator

Create engaging social media content to promote Oskris web design services in the Malaysian market.

## Platform Strategy

### Facebook (Primary — largest MY audience)
- **Format**: Image + long caption (100-300 words)
- **Frequency**: 3-4 posts/week
- **Best time**: 12pm-2pm, 8pm-10pm MYT
- **Content types**: Before/after, tips, client stories, behind-scenes
- **Language**: Mix of English + Bahasa Malaysia, occasional Chinese

### Instagram (Visual portfolio)
- **Format**: Carousel (most engagement), Reels, Stories
- **Frequency**: 4-5 posts/week
- **Best time**: 11am-1pm, 7pm-9pm MYT
- **Content types**: Design showcases, process reels, tips carousels
- **Hashtags**: 15-20 per post, mix of niche and broad

### TikTok (Growth channel)
- **Format**: Short video scripts (15-60 seconds)
- **Frequency**: 3-5 videos/week
- **Content types**: "Watch me build a website in 60s", tips, myths busted
- **Hook**: First 3 seconds must grab attention

## Content Pillars (Rotate Weekly)

1. **Showcase** (30%) — Portfolio pieces, before/after transformations
2. **Education** (25%) — Web design tips, "Did you know?", common mistakes
3. **Social Proof** (20%) — Client testimonials, results, case studies
4. **Behind the Scenes** (15%) — Process, tools, day-in-the-life
5. **Promotional** (10%) — Service offers, limited deals, CTAs

## Malaysian Market Rules
- Reference local brands, food, culture for relatability
- Use Manglish/casual tone for engagement posts
- Formal English for professional/educational content
- Include Bahasa Malaysia translations for key phrases
- Mention local payment methods (FPX, Boost, TNG) as features
- Reference Malaysian business challenges (competition, going digital)
