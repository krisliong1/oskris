# Email Templates

Professional email templates for every stage of the Oskris client relationship.

## Template Categories

### 1. Initial Contact
**First Response** (within 1 hour of inquiry):
```
Subject: Re: Website Inquiry — Oskris Web Design

Hi [Name],

Thank you for reaching out! I'm excited to learn about [business name].

I'd love to understand your goals better. Could you share:
1. What does [business] do?
2. Do you have a current website?
3. What's your ideal timeline?

I'm available for a quick chat anytime via WhatsApp at [number].

Looking forward to helping you grow online!

Best regards,
[Name]
Oskris Web Design
🌐 oskris.com | 📱 WhatsApp: [number]
```

## Email Rules
- Always include WhatsApp number as alternate contact
- Keep emails under 200 words (people don't read long emails)
- One clear CTA per email
- Professional but friendly tone
- Use client's name and business name
- Include Oskris branding in signature
- Adapt language based on client preference (EN/BM/CN)
